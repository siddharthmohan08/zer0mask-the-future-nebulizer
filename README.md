# zer0mask — Assembly & Hardware Guide

**Project status:** Experimental prototype

zer0mask is my idea for a futuristic nebulizer that is much more advanced than current nebulizer systems. I want it to be cheaper than current designs (20 dollars or less ideally) and I want it to use recycled parts in some way. It should also have the transparent design of the frutiger aero era.

It uses off-the-shelf humidifier parts, recycled e-cigarette batteries, and 3D printed parts made from food grade materials. I truly hope to bring this to market some day.

> **Important:** zer0mask is a research/prototyping project and has **not** been established as a clinically validated medical device. The instructions below describe the construction of the prototype hardware only. **Do not use an unvalidated prototype to administer medication to yourself or another person.** Electrical, material, aerosol, sterility, dosage-delivery, and regulatory testing are required before clinical use.

## Overview

The device integrates:

- A removable medicine reservoir
- An ultrasonic humidifier/nebulizer module
- A dedicated module mount
- A rechargeable battery
- A charging/power-management circuit
- A push-button power control
- A removable electronics tray
- An electronics-bay lid
- A mask/interface

The enclosure is designed so that the major components assemble without conventional screws or adhesives. Instead, the components use carefully sized friction-fit interfaces. This makes the device easier to assemble, disassemble, modify, and prototype.

## 3. Electrical Assembly

### 3.1 Charging Circuit

The charging circuit provides a controlled method of charging the rechargeable battery.

For a typical single-cell rechargeable battery system, the charging board will have terminals corresponding to the battery and charging input. The exact labels vary between boards.

A common arrangement is conceptually:

```
USB / CHARGING INPUT
        │
        ▼
┌──────────────────┐
│ Charging /       │
│ Protection Board │
└───────┬──────────┘
        │
        ▼
     BATTERY
```

Before soldering:

- Identify the positive and negative battery terminals.
- Identify the charging-board battery terminals.
- Verify the board is designed for the specific battery chemistry and cell configuration.
- Verify the output voltage required by the ultrasonic module.
- Confirm the battery's maximum permitted charging current.
- Confirm that the charging board provides the protection functions required by the battery.

Never connect a rechargeable lithium battery to an unknown charging circuit.

### 3.2 Connecting the Battery

Connect the battery to the charging circuit according to the charging-board manufacturer's pinout.

Conceptually:

```
Battery (+) ───────── Charger BAT+
Battery (-) ───────── Charger BAT-
```

The battery should be physically secured inside the electronics tray so that it cannot move freely during assembly or operation.

Avoid allowing:

- Bare conductors to contact the enclosure
- Solder joints to contact one another
- Battery leads to become pinched
- Sharp printed edges to contact the battery
- The battery to contact conductive components unintentionally

The battery should also remain isolated from areas where liquid could potentially accumulate.

## 4. Connecting the Ultrasonic Module

The ultrasonic module is responsible for converting electrical energy into aerosol-generating ultrasonic vibrations.

The module's electrical input must be connected according to its specified voltage and current requirements.

Conceptually:

```
Battery / Power System
        │
        ▼
   Push Button
        │
        ▼
Ultrasonic Module
```

The important design requirement is that the electrical system must supply the module with the voltage and current specified by the module manufacturer.

Do not connect the module directly to a battery simply because the connectors physically fit.

If the module requires a regulated voltage, an appropriate regulator must be incorporated into the power system.

## 5. Push-Button Installation

The push button is installed into the opening on the side of the electronics bay.

Its purpose is to provide the user with an accessible control for the electrical system.

The button is mechanically retained by the enclosure.

The electrical implementation depends on the specific switch being used. For a simple two-terminal switch, it can be incorporated into the positive power path:

```
Power (+)
   │
   ▼
[ PUSH BUTTON ]
   │
   ▼
Ultrasonic Module (+)

Power (-) ─────────────── Ultrasonic Module (-)
```

If the selected button is momentary rather than latching, additional control electronics may be necessary to achieve continuous operation.

## 6. Electronics Tray

The electronics tray is the removable internal structure that holds the electrical components.

Its primary functions are:

- Positioning the battery
- Supporting the charging circuit
- Organizing wiring
- Preventing components from moving inside the enclosure
- Allowing the electronics to be removed from the main body

Before installing the tray, complete and inspect the electrical assembly externally.

### Recommended assembly procedure

1. Install the battery into its designated location.
2. Secure the battery so it cannot shift.
3. Mount the charging circuit.
4. Route the wires toward their respective connection points.
5. Install the push-button wiring.
6. Connect the ultrasonic module wiring.
7. Inspect all solder joints.
8. Check for unintended electrical shorts.
9. Verify polarity.
10. Only after electrical inspection, place the completed tray into the electronics bay.

The wiring should have enough slack to prevent excessive stress on solder joints, but not so much slack that wires interfere with the friction-fit interfaces.

## 7. Electronics Bay

The electronics bay forms the central body of zer0mask.

It provides:

- Structural support for the electronics
- The push-button mounting location
- The interface between the electronics tray and the nebulizer mount
- The connection point for the upper mask assembly
- A physical barrier between the electronics and the fluid-handling components

The electronics tray slides into the bay.

Because the enclosure uses friction fits, the tray should seat firmly without requiring excessive force.

### Friction-fit principle

The tray should:

- Slide smoothly into the bay
- Remain securely positioned once installed
- Be removable by hand
- Not require permanent adhesive
- Not deform the electronics bay during installation

If excessive force is required, do not force the parts together. Check for:

- Printing artifacts
- Warping
- Elephant's foot
- Support material
- Incorrect print scaling
- Misalignment
- Excessive friction at one location

## 8. Electronics-Bay Lid

The electronics lid closes the upper portion of the electronics compartment.

Its primary purpose is to:

- Retain the electronics tray
- Protect the electronics
- Provide the mechanical interface for the mask
- Maintain the overall structure of the device

The lid is installed after the electronics tray has been placed into the electronics bay.


The lid should be completely seated before installing the mask.

## 9. Ultrasonic Module Mount

The ultrasonic module mount is positioned beneath the electronics bay.

Its job is to accurately locate the ultrasonic module relative to the reservoir and aerosol pathway.

The mount is designed to be a mechanical interface, rather than relying on adhesive.

The ultrasonic module should be inserted into the mount carefully, ensuring that:

- The module is fully seated.
- The active nebulizing surface is positioned correctly.
- No printed material interferes with the active surface.
- Electrical wires are routed away from the fluid path.
- The module does not wobble excessively.

The mount then friction-fits into the lower portion of the electronics enclosure.

## 10. Medicine Reservoir

The reservoir forms the lowest portion of the assembly.

It is designed to hold the liquid used by the nebulizing system and interface directly with the ultrasonic module.

The reservoir attaches to the bottom of the nebulizer mount using a friction-fit connection.

```
       NEBULIZER MOUNT
              │
              ▼
       ┌─────────────┐
       │ ULTRASONIC  │
       │   MODULE    │
       └──────┬──────┘
              │
              ▼
       ┌─────────────┐
       │  RESERVOIR  │
       │             │
       │   LIQUID    │
       │             │
       └─────────────┘
```

The reservoir should be removable so that it can be inspected and cleaned independently from the electronics.

### Fluid/electronics separation

One of the most important design considerations is keeping the fluid path physically separated from the electronics.

The reservoir and ultrasonic assembly should be inspected for leakage before connecting the electronics.

Do not operate the electrical system if liquid has entered the electronics compartment.

## 11. Mask Installation

The mask is the uppermost component of the device.

It attaches to the top of the electronics enclosure through its designated friction-fit interface.

Installation:

1. Ensure the electronics lid is completely seated.
2. Align the mask with the central outlet.
3. Press the mask downward evenly.
4. Confirm that the mask is fully seated.
5. Inspect the outlet for obstruction.

The mask should not be forced into place at an angle, as this can damage the friction-fit surfaces.

## 12. Complete Mechanical Assembly

Once all individual components have been prepared, the complete assembly sequence is:

**Step 1 — Prepare the electronics**

Assemble the battery, charging circuit, switch, and ultrasonic-module wiring according to the applicable component specifications.

**Step 2 — Install the electronics into the tray**

Place the completed electronics assembly into the removable electronics tray.

Ensure that the battery and circuit board are securely retained.

**Step 3 — Install the tray**

Slide the electronics tray into the electronics bay.

Make sure it is fully seated.

**Step 4 — Install the lid**

Place the electronics lid onto the top of the electronics bay.

Verify that the lid is fully engaged.

**Step 5 — Install the ultrasonic module**

Place the ultrasonic module into the dedicated mount.

Verify its orientation and seating.

**Step 6 — Attach the ultrasonic mount**

Connect the module mount to the bottom of the electronics enclosure.

**Step 7 — Attach the reservoir**

Insert the reservoir into the bottom of the ultrasonic mount.

Verify that the connection is fully seated.

**Step 8 — Install the mask**

Attach the mask to the top of the electronics enclosure.

## 13. Friction-Fit Design

One of the defining features of zer0mask is its tool-less mechanical assembly.

Rather than permanently bonding the components together, each interface is designed around controlled interference/friction.

### Advantages

This approach allows:

- Rapid prototyping
- Easy component replacement
- Electronics access
- Reservoir removal
- Easier cleaning and inspection
- Iterative CAD development
- Different versions of individual components to be tested without redesigning the entire device

### Important considerations

3D-printed friction fits are highly dependent on:

- Printer calibration
- Layer height
- Material
- Nozzle diameter
- Print orientation
- Temperature
- Dimensional accuracy
- Environmental temperature
- Part geometry

Consequently, the nominal CAD dimensions may not produce identical fits on every printer.

If an interface is too tight, adjust the CAD clearance rather than applying excessive force.

If an interface is too loose, increase the interference or add an appropriate retention feature in the next CAD revision.

## 14. Assembly Inspection Checklist

Before considering the mechanical assembly complete, inspect every interface.

### Mechanical

- [ ] Mask fully seated
- [ ] Electronics lid fully seated
- [ ] Electronics tray fully inserted
- [ ] Push button correctly positioned
- [ ] Ultrasonic module securely mounted
- [ ] Reservoir fully seated
- [ ] No excessive gaps between components
- [ ] No cracked printed parts
- [ ] No sharp edges interfering with assembly
- [ ] All friction-fit connections can be removed without damaging the parts

### Electrical

- [ ] Battery polarity verified
- [ ] Charging circuit verified for the selected battery
- [ ] Ultrasonic module voltage verified
- [ ] Wiring inspected
- [ ] Solder joints inspected
- [ ] No exposed conductors can short against the enclosure
- [ ] Battery physically secured
- [ ] Charging behavior verified independently
- [ ] No abnormal heating observed during controlled electrical testing

### Fluid path

- [ ] Reservoir inspected for defects
- [ ] Ultrasonic module correctly positioned
- [ ] Fluid path unobstructed
- [ ] Electronics physically separated from liquid-containing components
- [ ] No evidence of leakage

## 15. Design Philosophy

zer0mask was designed around a simple principle:

> Separate the device into modular mechanical and electrical systems that can be independently modified and tested.

The result is a vertically stacked architecture:

**Interface → enclosure → electronics → aerosol-generation system → reservoir**

This arrangement allows individual sections to be redesigned without completely rebuilding the device.

For example, future versions could replace the:

- Mask without redesigning the electronics
- Electronics tray without changing the reservoir
- Reservoir without redesigning the upper enclosure
- Ultrasonic module mount to accommodate a different module
- Battery/electronics package while maintaining the same external form factor

This modularity is particularly useful during prototyping because the performance of individual subsystems can be evaluated independently.

## Prototype Safety Notice

zer0mask is an experimental respiratory-device prototype. The fact that the device produces visible aerosol does not establish that it delivers medication at a safe, accurate, or therapeutically appropriate dose.

Before any human-use application, the design would require appropriate engineering and medical-device validation, including evaluation of aerosol characteristics, delivered dose, materials compatibility, contamination/sterility, electrical and battery safety, fluid-path safety, reliability, and applicable regulatory requirements.

For development and demonstration, testing should use non-drug test fluids appropriate for the test method rather than medication.
